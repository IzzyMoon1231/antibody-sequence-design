ANTIBODY-FORMAT ABLATION
------------------------
A formal VHH-versus-scFv ablation was not performed because the
available dataset contains only one experimental complex for each
format (9NH7 VHH and 9NFU scFv).

Although 100 designs were analyzed per method for each complex, these
are repeated designs of the same underlying antibody-antigen systems,
not independent VHH or scFv complexes. Therefore, differences between
9NH7 and 9NFU cannot be attributed specifically to antibody format.


INTERPRETATION NOTE
-------------------
The structural analysis measures framework-aligned C-alpha
contact-residue positional preservation relative to the experimental
complex. Because the ESMFold predictions do not contain the antigen,
these results estimate preservation of original interface geometry
and should not be interpreted as direct evidence of preserved binding.
